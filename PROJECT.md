**Brief description (English):** An original multiplayer arcade racer where up to eight friends drift and boost through Pine Ridge’s forest circuit. Join a private room, race in real time, and chase the finish line together.

**Full description:**

Ridge Run is an original multiplayer arcade racer set on Pine Ridge, a winding forest circuit.
Invite up to seven friends to a private room and race together in real time.
Every driver launches from the same grid and chases the same finish line.
Choose a bright car color and follow the live leaderboard as racers change positions.
Steer through flowing bends, sharper corners, roadside barriers, and mountain scenery.
Build speed with the accelerator and use handbrake drifts to carve through turns.
Fire nitro for a burst of speed when you want to catch the pack or protect your lead.
Use keyboard controls on desktop or large touch controls on a phone in landscape mode.
Reconnect to your room and continue from your saved race position if your connection drops.
Practice solo or host a private race with synchronized movement, countdown, and finish times.
